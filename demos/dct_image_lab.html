Reading the DCT

Each real coefficient weights a cosine basis image. DC is the image's constant component; coefficients farther from the upper left represent finer variations. Signed log reveals weaker values while preserving their signs. Linear shows proportional values.

Try this

Choose the centered white square, apply a low-pass mask, and undo. Then generate vertical cosine bands at mode 8: one coefficient at (u,v) = (8,0), plus DC, creates the whole pattern. Set a low-pass radius of 0 to keep only the image's mean.

Transform convention

This is the orthonormal 2D DCT-II of the entire 128 × 128 image. Its cosine basis corresponds to an even reflection across image boundaries. Coefficients stay in their natural order, starting at DC.

Translation experiments

Image mode moves the pixels and recomputes the DCT. DCT mode moves coefficient bins and reconstructs the image. Both translations wrap around their grids; changing coefficient positions creates different cosine patterns.
